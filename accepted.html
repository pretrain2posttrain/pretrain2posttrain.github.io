Accepted papers instructions

Instructions for authors of accepted papers. Every accepted paper is presented as a poster; a subset is additionally selected for an oral presentation.

Posters

  • Poster sessionsTwo sessions, each one hour
  • PrintingOrder a printed poster via PosterSessionOnline
  • NeurIPS guidanceOfficial NeurIPS 2026 poster instructions

Note the portrait orientation and the workshop-specific dimensions — they differ from the main-conference poster size.

  • Length15 minutes per talk
  • Format12-minute talk followed by 3 minutes of questions
